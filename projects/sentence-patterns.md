# Prison Sentence Patterns

**CS 617 · Statistical Computing · Fall 2022**  
**Collaborative coursework**

## Analysis question

How do recorded sentence lengths and age distributions differ across state-level categories?

## Workflow and interpretation

- Worked with sentence-period records from [Prison Statistics India](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rajanand/prison-in-india).
- Used R, dplyr and ggplot2 for filtering and visualization. The coursework focused on ages 18–30 and examined sentence length and state groupings.
- Sentence-period counts describe the records covered by the dataset. Comparing crime rates across states would require population denominators and comparable reporting coverage. An imposed sentence and time actually served are different measures.
