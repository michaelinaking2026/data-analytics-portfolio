# Prison Release and Recidivism

**CS 628 · Data Science · Fall 2022**  
**Collaborative coursework**

## Analysis question

How do reported release and recidivism patterns vary across fiscal years and groups?

## Workflow and interpretation

- Used Python and R for exploratory statistical analysis and visualization. The coursework also discussed decision trees.
- Compared fiscal-year release patterns and explored variation across groups. The report identifies [Prison Statistics India](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rajanand/prison-in-india) as a source. The provenance of certain subgroup figures is unclear, so the case study focuses on the analytical approach.
- A defensible recidivism rate requires a consistent definition of the outcome, an eligible release cohort as the denominator and a defined follow-up period. Changes in release counts alone cannot establish a change in recidivism or an effect of rehabilitation programs.
